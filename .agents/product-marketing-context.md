## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Homeowners in rural Southern TN / Northern AL struggle to find a licensed contractor who is genuinely local, hands-on, transparent about pricing, and accountable from quote to completion.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Production builders won't customize for your lot or lifestyle
- Larger builders hand you off to project managers — you never meet the owner
- Some contractors push full replacements when a repair would do
- Out-of-area crews don't understand local soil, terrain, or climate

**What it costs them:** Wasted money on unnecessary work, homes that don't fit the land, roofs that fail in 15 years, septic systems that fail inspection or back up.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of being taken advantage of on the biggest purchase of their life. Anxiety about making the wrong call on technical decisions (metal vs. shingle, repair vs. replace).
